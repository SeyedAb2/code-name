از هیچ‌چی نمی‌دانم تا سروری که
خودت نگهش می‌داری

From knowing nothing to running
the server yourself

این‌ها دورهٔ ویدیویی نیستند. مستند مرجع‌اند — مثل صفحه‌های راهنمای رسمی، اما فارسی، با مثال خودمانی، تمرین با پاسخ کامل، و سه پروژهٔ واقعی در پایان هر مسیر.

These are not video courses. They are reference documents — like official manuals, but in plain language, with worked examples, exercises that ship with full solutions, and three real projects at the end of every track.

Codenameh is free and will stay free — no ads, no subscription, no tracking. If it helped and you would like to support it, a direct card transfer is the only option for now; there is no payment gateway yet. Nothing is required, and no content is ever put behind a paywall.
